About

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Gothenburg, supervised by Simon Larson and Jeffrey Steif.

Before moving to Gothenburg, I completed an M.Sc. in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics at LMU Munich and the Technical University of Munich, as well as B.Sc. degrees in Mathematics and Physics at the University of Göttingen.

Research

My research interests lie in spectral theory, with a focus on spectral asymptotics and the analytical methods used to study them. I am particularly interested in the influence of boundary regularity on spectral properties of the Laplacian, as well as in developing techniques for obtaining asymptotic expansions. I am also interested in related questions for Schrödinger operators.

01

Spectral Asymptotics

Studying the asymptotic behavior of spectral quantities, including heat traces, Riesz means, and the relationships between them.

02

Boundary Effects

Investigating how boundary geometry and regularity influence spectral properties, particularly for the Laplacian on Lipschitz domains.

03

Analytical Methods

Developing and applying analytical techniques, including heat kernel and Tauberian methods, to establish spectral asymptotics.
